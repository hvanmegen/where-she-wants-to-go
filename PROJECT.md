# Where She Wants To Go

Cyberpunk 2077 fan-fiction novella. Intimate romantic tragedy about Mariyana "Mari" Novak, Aleksandra "Sanya" Velez, and Mari's twin Elias "Eli" Novak: love, embodiment, grief, autonomy, sensation, and digital existence.

## Absolute core

- **CANON:** Story runs Sep 2078–Jul 2080. Mari and Sanya become a couple on 7–8 Feb 2079; Eli dies 29 Apr 2080; Sanya transitions 16 Jul 2080.
- **CANON:** Eli and Sanya have PNASD, incurable progressive neuroimmune disease. Mari does not have a known diagnosis.
- **CANON:** Mari meets Sanya in a disease clinic while Eli lives. Sanya is Mari's first woman partner.
- **CANON:** Sanya is a medical-systems netrunner. Her augmentations conceal severe sensory/autonomic decline; cyberspace restores embodiment.
- **CANON:** Eli is an Arasaka neurointerface validation researcher. His death is strongly suspected suicide, never confirmed.
- **CANON:** Sanya's destructive, permanent digital transition preserves emotionally meaningful but metaphysically ambiguous continuity. Mari lets her choose, then grieves her bodily loss.
