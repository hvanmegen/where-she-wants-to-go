# Embodiment

Sanya's body is hers, beautiful, pleasurable, unreliable, and temporary. Chrome preserves sensation while exposing its fragility. Digital embodiment is experiential, not avatar cosmetics. Strong motif: she learned to wear a body like a fever meant to pass.
