# Love and harm

Love can be sincere while actions taken in its name remain harmful. Love may explain a violation; it does not excuse it, make it selfless, or create an automatic right to forgiveness.

## Character expressions

- Mari loves Sanya, but grief can turn attention into surveillance and care into control.
- Nadia loves Sanya as chosen family, but may violate Mari's trust and Eli's privacy to secure Sanya's escape.
- Sanya loves Mari, but conceals her decline and limits Mari's chance to prepare.
- Eli loves Mari, but his possible choice to hasten death leaves her with uncertainty and guilt.

## Writing guardrails

- Do not imply harmful behavior proves love was false.
- Do not use love as moral absolution.
- Preserve agency, consequences, anger, and uncertain forgiveness.
- Let conflict and behavior express this philosophy; avoid characters delivering it as a thesis.

Guiding formulation: **Love explains the terrible thing. It does not excuse it.**
