# Cyberspace

For Sanya, cyberspace restores embodiment: touch, pressure, temperature, proprioception, movement, pleasure, and confidence. It is neither addiction nor rival girlfriend. Mari initially thinks Sanya is leaving her body; truth: body is leaving Sanya.

Mari and Sanya can meet in private full-sensory shared space. Shared time normally runs at real-time pace. Mari must disconnect and return to physical world alone.
