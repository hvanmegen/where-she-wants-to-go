# Mikoshi / Blackwall escape

## Provisional architecture

- Arasaka initially instantiates Sanya inside a surviving or reconstructed Mikoshi-derived partition, where its ownership claim can be enforced.
- Mikoshi is not beyond the Blackwall. Nadia's route would take Sanya **out of** controlled Mikoshi storage through a temporary Blackwall breach, then into the distributed habitat.
- Nadia derives the route from Eli's validation telemetry, cryptographic traces, or documentation of an obscure maintenance/export path.
- Mari grants Nadia limited access to Eli's files to help Sanya. Nadia secretly copies more than permitted and repurposes it, betraying Mari's trust and Eli's privacy.
- Nadia acts from a parental chosen-family bond with Sanya and accepts moral, legal, and personal costs Mari did not consent to.
- Alt Cunningham functions as technical precedent and netrunner legend, not personal rescuer. Nadia knows considerably more than Mari or Sanya, but has no reliable relationship with Alt.

## Intended dramatic result

Nadia can open the route, but Sanya must cross it. Contact may disappear during transit, leaving Mari unable to know whether Sanya survived. Sanya's successful emergence defeats Arasaka's confinement without restoring her body or resolving identity continuity.

## Open

Whether this route becomes canon; status of Mikoshi after 2077; exact breach mechanics and risks; what Sanya knows beforehand; when Mari learns of Nadia's theft; whether crossing changes or damages Sanya.
