# Digital habitat

Nadia's prebuilt escape route migrates Sanya from Arasaka infrastructure to encrypted distributed nodes immediately after stabilization. Habitat supports private full-sensory shared space and resists deletion; it does not guarantee invulnerability or eliminate corporate leverage.
