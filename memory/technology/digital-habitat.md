# Digital habitat

Nadia's prebuilt escape route migrates Sanya from Arasaka infrastructure to encrypted distributed nodes immediately after stabilization, averting corporate confinement. Habitat supports private full-sensory shared space and resists deletion; it does not guarantee invulnerability, eliminate corporate leverage, restore Sanya's body, or turn survival into an uncomplicated victory.
