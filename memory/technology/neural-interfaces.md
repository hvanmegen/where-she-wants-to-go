# Neural interfaces

Cyberpunk-compatible story extrapolation: Sanya's custom port and sensory rig route synthetic feedback around damaged pathways, remapping some input to less-affected neural territory. Her extensive neck/spine/brainstem hardware prolongs sensation but conceals severe disease and increases dependence.

Eli validates signal reliability/accessibility at Arasaka. This is foundational, indirect contribution—not personal miracle technology. Mari has minimal chrome and enters shared cyberspace through borrowed medical-grade rig.

Primary ongoing cost: extended linking can leave Sanya's physical body unattended and mask decline. Conflict is autonomy versus care, not addiction.

## Biometal

**CANON, original extrapolation:** Biometal is adaptive, tissue-integrated morphic metal used in refined chrome such as Sanya’s neck interface. It flexes and conforms with living structures while housing or supporting interface components. It is not presently treated as an established Cyberpunk lore term. Exact composition remains open.
