## Mariyana "Mari" Novak

**Appearance — CANON:** 29; warm olive-to-light-brown skin; dark-brown hair, usually in a loose messy bun; soft, grounded features; tired but gentle expression; minimal visible augmentation. Her facial appearance visibly reflects both Japanese and Latino ancestry rather than reading predominantly as one or the other. Red and amber light emphasize her warmth and physical presence.

**Ethnicity — CANON:** Latino-Japanese heritage, shared with her twin, [Eli](eli-novak.md). Exact family migration history, nationality, and degree of connection to each culture remain open.

## Aleksandra "Sanya" Velez

**Appearance — CANON:** 28; pale skin; short dark hair with a blunt fringe; angular, expressive face; refined implant seams across temples and cheeks; extensive visible augmentation through neck, shoulders, arms, spine, and brainstem pathways. Her adaptive-biometal neck interface is prominent. Cyan and blue light emphasize her cooler, digitally oriented presence.

**Ethnicity — CANON:** French-Russian heritage. Exact family migration history, nationality, and degree of connection to each culture remain open. She grew up in an unnamed Midwestern megacity before moving to Night City.

## Elias "Eli" Novak

**Appearance — CANON:** 29, six minutes younger than [Mari](mari-novak.md); warm olive-to-light-brown complexion; dense, short, curly dark-brown hair; dark-brown eyes; softly angular face; straight brows; slim nose; full lips; lean build; faint shadows beneath his eyes; no prominent facial cyberware. His appearance reads more strongly Hispanic/Latino than Mari's, while her features show a more visibly blended Japanese-Latino heritage. They retain clear family resemblance. Eli looks thinner, quieter, and more visibly tired; his appearance must show a person living with illness, not reduce him to illness.

**Ethnicity — CANON:** Latino-Japanese heritage, shared with Mari. Exact family migration history, nationality, and degree of connection to each culture remain open.

## Nadia Kirova

**Appearance — OPEN:** No canonical appearance yet.

**Ethnicity — CANON:** French-Chinese heritage. Exact nationality, family migration history, and degree of connection to each culture remain open. Her surname does not override this heritage.
