# Elias "Eli" Novak

- **CANON:** 29, exactly the same age as Mari except for being six minutes younger. Warm olive-to-light-brown complexion; dense, short, curly dark-brown hair; dark-brown eyes; softly angular face; straight brows; slim nose; full lips; lean build. His appearance reads more strongly Hispanic/Latino than Mari's, despite their shared Latino-Japanese ancestry. He often looks quietly tired or pensive, with faint shadows beneath his eyes. No prominent facial cyberware is visible. He resembles Mari as her fraternal twin but looks thinner and sicker. Dry, stubborn, quietly warm.
- **CANON:** Latino-Japanese heritage, shared with Mari. Exact family migration history, nationality, and degree of connection to each culture remain open.
- **CANON:** PNASD diagnosis shaped his education. He became a neurointerface validation researcher, seeking to understand and help people like himself.
- **CANON:** Both he and Sanya applied to [Arasaka Academy](../locations/arasaka-academy.md); only Eli received scholarship. They later become clinic acquaintances. Sanya initially resents the comparison.
- **CANON:** Starts Arasaka research role 1 Aug 2079. Learns terminal brain involvement 15 Dec; Mari knows. Dies 29 Apr 2080, strongly suspected suicide but never confirmed.

## Meaning

Eli fights bodily decline through knowledge. His validation work becomes an indirect foundation for Sanya's later viable transition, but system maturity arrives too late for him.
