# Aleksandra "Sanya" Velez

- **CANON:** 28. Pale; short dark hair with blunt fringe; refined, extensive visible hardware across temples, cheeks, neck, shoulders, arms, spine, and brainstem pathways. Earlier names **Saryn, Sanni, Seren, and others are SUPERSEDED**.
- **CANON:** French-Russian heritage. Exact family migration history, nationality, and degree of connection to each culture remain open.
- **CANON:** Medical-systems netrunner. In free time she helps people with similar illness record braindances of more vivid former neural selves. She stopped dangerous field work because her body could no longer safely run or escape.
- **CANON:** PNASD began showing in teen years. Parents hid diagnosis before symptoms to protect childhood; she experienced this as betrayal, left their unnamed Midwest megacity for Night City, and maintains low unresolved contact.
- **CANON:** Moderate disease was visible at first meeting; severe sensory/autonomic progression and illegal bypass load were hidden from Mari. Chrome remaps dwindling sensation onto less-affected pathways, eventually via a neck/spine/brainstem rig.
- **CANON:** She loves, resents, enjoys, and cannot trust her body. She is not suicidal or emotionally flat about death.

## Arc

Cyberspace moves from work to freedom, restored sensation, identity, then chosen continued existence. She secretly falls for Mari at first sight; prose never states this outright. Her later transition is not rejection of Mari.
