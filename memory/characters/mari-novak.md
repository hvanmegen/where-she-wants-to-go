# Mariyana "Mari" Novak

- **CANON:** 29. Warm olive-to-light-brown skin; dark-brown hair usually in loose messy bun; softer, grounded, tired-gentle presence. Her facial appearance visibly reflects both her Japanese and Latino ancestry rather than reading predominantly as one or the other. Minimal practical chrome, no permanent neural interface.
- **CANON:** Latino-Japanese heritage. Exact family migration history, nationality, and degree of connection to each culture remain open.
- **CANON:** Neighborhood mobile-care medic. Childhood hospital visits with Eli made care familiar. Trauma Team rejected her as a poor fit for corporate combat triage: she would not abandon low-priority patients for contract metrics.
- **CANON:** Twin of [Eli](eli-novak.md), six minutes older; calls him her little brother. Their parents medicalized Eli's whole life; siblings are estranged from them.
- **CANON:** Dated men before Sanya; one serious ex-boyfriend stays backstory-only. Her label remains intentionally unstated.

## Arc

After Eli's death, protection of [Sanya](sanya-velez.md) risks becoming control. She learns physical presence is not sole form of presence and love does not grant ownership over Sanya's survival choice.
