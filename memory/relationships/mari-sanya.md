# Mari / Sanya

## Canon progression

Mari meets Sanya Sep 2078 at clinic after Sanya unknowingly snags a sensor lead. They become a couple across Tue–Wed, 7–8 Feb 2079. Sanya is Mari's first woman partner. Their warmth, sex, jokes, work, and ordinary intimacy must be real before Eli's death; sexual prose is sensual but non-graphic.

Sanya's pre-existing slight resentment of Eli changes when she meets Mari; her immediate attraction remains subtext. Mari learns of Eli's Arasaka start while relationship is early.

## Central conflict

Mari knows Sanya has PNASD but not its true severity or bypass load. Eli's death makes Mari treat every symptom as prophecy. Sanya refuses to become Eli, a medical project, or a body kept alive for Mari's comfort.

## Reciprocal betrayal — canon direction

Sanya conceals her severe progression and illegal bypass load because she fears disclosure will change Mari's gaze: lover will become caregiver, and intimacy will become surveillance. Mari experiences that concealment as betrayal because Sanya allowed her to believe they had more time and a more truthful shared future.

Mari's answering betrayal is turning access into authority. Grief makes her monitor, pressure, and restrict Sanya while calling it care. Sanya experiences this as confirmation that trusting someone with her vulnerable body gives them a claim over it.

Both act partly to protect their relationship and instead produce the outcome the other feared. Sanya's secrecy leaves Mari feeling abandoned before Sanya has gone; Mari's control leaves Sanya feeling abandoned while Mari is still holding her. Neither betrayal comes from diminished love.

## Resolution

Sanya's transition is permanent, destructive, and chosen. Mari later tries one Doll session with her; consent cannot make another person's body a substitute. Mari still chooses connection and lets Sanya go.
