# Mari / Eli

Twins, Mari six minutes older. "Little brother" is their affectionate absurdity. Estranged parents' medical surveillance made their sibling bond more self-reliant. Mari sees his Arasaka role as hope.

Eli tells Mari his terminal prognosis. After his death, deleted-message fragments and missing medication suggest he took fate into his own hands, but no proof resolves it. Mari's grief later distorts care for Sanya.
