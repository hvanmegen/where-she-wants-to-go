# Mari meets Sanya

At a disease-linked clinic in Sep 2078, Sanya unknowingly snags a clinic sensor lead because she cannot feel it catch. Mari is there for Eli and helps. Eli and Sanya already recognize each other as clinic acquaintances after their Arasaka Academy history.

This is an ordinary-life symptom, not medical exposition. Mari reads inconvenience; later, after Eli dies, same kind of moment reads as terror.
