# Eli joins Arasaka

On 1 Aug 2079, Eli begins his hard-won Arasaka neurointerface validation role. Mari tells Sanya while their relationship is still early and sees it as hope.

His work is an original, Arasaka-compatible research function—not an asserted official canon department. Its validation data later helps make Sanya's neural map viable, but project maturity comes too late for Eli.
