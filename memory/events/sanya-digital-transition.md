# Sanya's digital transition

On 16 Jul 2080, Sanya undergoes irreversible destructive neural scan because late brainstem disease and bypass load are closing viable-map window. Biological body dies. Continuity is metaphysically ambiguous; Sanya's experienced and relational continuity is treated as meaningful.

Arasaka's ownership terms become a major late obstacle under the pressure of Sanya's closing medical deadline. Nadia executes a prebuilt escape route immediately after stabilization, moving Sanya to distributed habitat before Arasaka can confine her. The escape succeeds: Sanya belongs to herself. That win does not return her body, resolve continuity, or spare Mari's grief.
