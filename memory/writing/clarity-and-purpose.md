# Clarity and purpose

- Name acting character after any plausible ownership shift; use pronouns only through unbroken, unambiguous action.
- Atmospheric detail must establish place, character, tension, motif, or later payoff. Remove isolated activity that invites explanation but has none.
- Track physical objects and positions through scene; do not let bags, leads, screens, or bodies reset invisibly.
- Let early dialogue reveal worldview without stating story's final thematic lesson before characters earn it.
- Complete scene turns: encounter, interruption, consequence, and changed relationship state.
