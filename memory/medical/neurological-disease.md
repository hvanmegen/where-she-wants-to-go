# Progressive Neuroimmune Autonomic-Sensory Degeneration (PNASD)

## Canon model

Fictional, medically grounded autoimmune disease. It attacks sensory/autonomic ganglia first, causing unreliable touch, temperature, proprioception, grip, balance, fatigue, and autonomic dysfunction. Later CNS spread reaches brainstem, then higher central networks, producing speech problems, cognitive decline, terminal neurological deterioration, and loss of viable neural-map integrity.

Standard care manages symptoms and fluctuations but cannot stop progression. Individual courses vary: Eli's terminal CNS change becomes evident in late 2079; Sanya has concealed more advanced loss with illegal sensory remapping chrome.

## Continuity rules

Track symptom geography, mobility, fatigue, autonomic risk, and interface efficacy. Show through ordinary consequence. Sanya can remain cognitively capable until transition, but delay threatens map viability.
